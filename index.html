Insights

What the numbers taught me

Patterns from comparing campaigns side by side, in both main roles.

E-Gurus

Better buyers, not just more clicks

At 40dash, conversion rate rose 26% across the period. I work on who sees the ads and where they land, so more visitors buy.

E-Gurus

Catalog sets the efficiency bar

The Advantage+ catalog campaign returned 5.16x at EGP 189 per purchase. I compare every creative-led campaign against it before adding budget.

Cherry & Brush

Volume and efficiency are different jobs

The reminder campaign brought 435 purchases at 4.8x. Ramadan offers hit 9.7x on only 42. I use the first to scale and the second as a signal of what the market wants.

Cherry & Brush

UGC wins, with the right offer

Two UGC campaigns returned 7.5x and 6.8x. A third returned 4.1x, so format alone isn't enough. Timing and offer matter as much.

Cherry & Brush

Plan for the week after the peak

Pink Friday and Ramadan made the biggest months, then April fell 57%. I now plan the weeks after each event, not only the event.

Cherry & Brush

Bundles raise the basket

Orders fell after March, but average order value climbed from EGP 2,592 to EGP 2,848 by May, helped by bundles and installment offers.
