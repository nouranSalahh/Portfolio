Campaign Strategy

Seasonal campaign timeline

Key campaign phases managed throughout the engagement.

Oct – Nov 2025

Pink Friday / Black Friday Campaigns

Launched ABO and CBO structures for the Pink Friday sale. Managed the "Sales_pink friday" series across broad audiences with product bundles and installment offers. Drove EGP 35.5M in November gross sales.

Nov – Dec 2025

November Shoot + Installment Focus

Ran CBO November shoot, CBO hot-air tri-dry, and installment-specific campaigns. Tested influencer creative via a CBO-Influencer structure. Implemented retargeting layers alongside prospecting to maximize CVR.

Dec 2025 – Jan 2026

Year-End Sales Push

Scaled ABO_reminder to 435 purchases and EGP 1M+ conversion value. Launched UGC-focused creatives and micro-influencer ABO. Best CPP of EGP 327 on UGC-Existing.

Feb 2026

Valentine's Day Campaigns

Ran 8 Valentine's campaigns including FOMO messaging, UGC content, installment offers, and video formats across multiple creative angles.

Mar – Apr 2026

Ramadan + Mother's Day Scale-Up

Best ad period on record — EGP 17.46M in March (+327%). Mother's Day UGC hit 306 purchases at 6.8× ROAS. Ramadan offers achieved 9.7× ROAS on a lean EGP 16K spend.

May – Jun 2026

Recovery & Optimization

May rebounded to EGP 11.98M (+52% MoM) with an improved AOV of EGP 2,848. Currently managing 9 active campaigns across the Cherry and B, and C&B-Cairo ad accounts.
